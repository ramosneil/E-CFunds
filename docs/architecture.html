Three flows, step by step

A student pays dues

  1. Treasurer taps Record payment on the member.App calls the record_payment function.
  2. Database checks the treasurer's org, marks the dues paid, adds a collection to the ledger and issues a receipt number.
  3. A notification row is created for the student.pg_net → push function → Firebase / Web Push.
  4. A live tick goes out; every open fund card shows the new cash on hand in about 2 seconds.

Money moves through SAS

  1. Treasurer files a remittance, release or expense (expenses need a receipt photo).
  2. SAS sees it under Approvals and gets a notification.
  3. SAS approves: the database checks the pockets and writes the ledger entry.Cash on hand and saved with SAS change together.
  4. The treasurer is notified; the "Waiting for SAS" note clears.

Activity budget and liquidation

  1. Treasurer files a budget with items; SAS approves it.
  2. Releases and expenses are tagged to the budget; it shows Released, Spent, Remaining.
  3. Within 15 days after the activity, the treasurer liquidates with a report and up to 10 files.
  4. SAS approves: unspent cash goes back to savings with SAS automatically.

What keeps it safe

Rules in the databaseRow Level Security and checked functions: a treasurer can only touch their own org.
Ledger is append-onlyMistakes are fixed with a correction entry, never by editing or deleting.
Audit logEvery change to money, accounts and budgets is recorded with who and when.
Secrets stay on the serverApps only hold the public key; service keys live in Supabase and GitHub secrets.
Private filesReceipts and liquidation files open through short-lived links, only for that org and SAS.
Session safetyApp logs out after 5 minutes away; sign-in tries are limited.
