Use E-CFunds like an app on your iPhone

Free — no App Store needed. It opens full screen from your Home Screen and can send you notifications (iPhone with iOS 16.4 or newer).

  1. 1
    Open Safari and go toe-cfunds.vercel.app
    Use Safari — not Chrome or Facebook's browser.
  2. 2
    Tap Share ⇧ The square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen (on iPad: at the top).
  3. 3
    Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add” Scroll down the list if you don't see it right away.
  4. 4
    Open E-CFunds from your Home Screen and sign in Use your Student ID and password, or Google.
  5. 5
    Turn on notifications Tap your profile icon (top right) → My account → Turn on notifications → Allow.
In E-CFunds you can see your dues, and every org and club's report: how much was collected, what it was spent on, and the approved expenses — open Reports.
